About

Each sheet, panel and inset has its own Helmert fit to Multnomah County tax lots; downtown sheets where Sanborn drew streets off width use one fit per block. Nothing here is hosted: the page reads IIIF Georeference Annotations and fetches the scans from LoC. Data and method: GitHub.
